What this product is

Domain Auth Fix-it is a DNS authentication health checker. You submit a sending domain; your browser looks up public DNS records (MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, BIMI) via DNS-over-HTTPS and shows a score plus a free Top 3 summary. DNS health ≠ inbox guarantee. We do not send test mail, measure inbox placement, or promise open rates.

Data we process

  • Domain you submit — used only to query public DNS and generate a report. On this static site, the check runs in your browser.
  • Optional DKIM selector — if you provide one, that selector name is probed.
  • Last-checked domain in your browser — may be stored in localStorage so the thanks or cancel page can mention it. You can clear it with your browser data.
  • Payment metadata (via Creem) — when checkout is enabled, Creem processes payment. The full Fix-it report is then emailed to the address used at checkout. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Technical logs — GitHub Pages, DNS-over-HTTPS resolvers (Cloudflare / Google public resolvers), and the payment processor may keep ordinary request logs (for example IP, user agent, timestamps) for reliability and abuse prevention.

What we do not collect for this MVP

We do not require an account to run a free check. This static host does not keep a server-side report store or /r/[id] share link. We do not sell your domain checks. We do not use submitted domains to send you marketing email unless you separately opt in (not part of this MVP).

Processors

Hosting (GitHub Pages), DNS-over-HTTPS providers (Cloudflare / Google public resolvers), and Creem (when Live checkout is wired) may process data as needed to operate the service.
